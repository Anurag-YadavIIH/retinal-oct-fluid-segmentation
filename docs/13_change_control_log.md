| 2026-09-17 | **HD95 definition corrected — values change.** **Old definition:** pool the two directed surface-distance sets into one distribution and take its 95th percentile, i.e. `percentile(concat(d(A→B), d(B→A)), 95)`. **New definition:** take the 95th percentile of each direction separately and return the larger, i.e. `max(percentile(d(A→B), 95), percentile(d(B→A), 95))`. Surfaces are the mask minus its connectivity-1 erosion; distances are spacing-aware and in millimetres; both-empty returns 0.0 and exactly-one-empty returns NaN, unchanged. | SRS-032, RC-006 | **How it was found:** TC-120, the back-to-back check against MONAI, disagreed on HD95 for 5 of 12 random mask pairs while Dice agreed on all 12. Computing both candidate definitions by hand showed max-of-directed reproducing MONAI exactly on the failing seeds. **Why max-of-directed is standard:** it is what MONAI, medpy and the segmentation literature compute, so it is what an HD95 reported for RETOUCH here can be compared against. The pooled version produced numbers that looked reasonable and were comparable to nothing published — a worse failure than an obviously wrong value, because nothing about it invites checking. **Why max-of-directed is also the better measure:** the two directions answer different questions — how far predicted boundary is from reference (false-positive excursions) and how far reference is from predicted (missed regions). Pooling averages them into one distribution, so a large error in one direction is diluted by a well-matched other direction whenever that direction contributes more surface voxels. A prediction that follows the reference closely everywhere except one under-segmented region therefore scores better than it should, which is precisely the failure HAZ-001 describes. Taking the max refuses that dilution: the metric is as bad as its worse direction. **Scope of the change:** no result had been produced under the old definition, so nothing needed recomputing. Had any been published, every HD95 figure in `docs/10` would have required regeneration | Anurag Yadav |
